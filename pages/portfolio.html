Rewire Fitness

Focus : Mental Performance & Cognitive Recovery

Platform : iOS & Android

App : Rewire Fitness – Mental Fitness App

Summary : Rewire is a mental fitness app used by high performers and athletes. It offers daily readiness assessments, guided neuro-training (visualization, focus drills), and recovery tools like breathing and journaling. Built for tracking resilience and optimizing mental sharpness.

02

HeadCoach

Focus : Performance Psychology & Coaching Tools

Platform : Internal/B2B

App : HeadCoach App

Summary : A private coaching app offering structured performance coaching for athletes and executives. It includes goal setting, habit trackers, session logging, and audio guidance from certified coaches. Ideal for team integrationand mindset coaching.

03

Therasize

Focus : Therapist–Client Engagement

Platform : Prototype/Private

App : Therasize – Therapy Companion

Summary : Therasize supports therapists with remote client engagement. Features include assigning custom worksheets (CBT tools, journaling), tracking assessments like PHQ-9 and GAD-7, and sending therapeutic prompts between sessions—HIPAA-compliant and clinician-ready.

04

CBT Hypnotherapy – MentalFuture

Focus : CBT & Hypnotherapy Self-Care

Platform : iOS

App : CBT Hypnotherapy – Mental Future

Summary : This app offers cognitive behavioral therapy and hypnotherapy modules for anxiety, sleep, confidence, and trauma. Includes mood tracking, audio sessions, affirmations, and in-app journaling to support therapeutic progress.

05

Mindleap

Focus : Psychedelic Integration & Mental Health Coaching

Platform : iOS & Android

App : Mindleap – Psychedelic Wellness

Summary : Mindleap is built for psychedelic-assisted therapy and mental wellness coaching. It connects users with certified guides, offers mood tracking and session notes, and provides a safe, private environment for integration support.

06

MamaLift

Focus : Perinatal & Maternal Mental Health

Platform : Android

App : Mamalift Plus

Summary : MamaLift is designed for mothers dealing with postpartum depression, anxiety, or emotional shifts. It offers CBT-based mood tracking, reflection tools, journaling, and therapeutic interventions tailored to perinatal wellness.

07

Goally

Focus : Life Skills & Behavioral Tools for Kids

Platform : iOS & Android

App : Goally

Summary : Goally helps children (especially those with ADHD, Autism, or developmental delays) through visual routines, reward charts, and guided habit-building. It reduces parental stress and fosters child independence in a structured, playful way.
